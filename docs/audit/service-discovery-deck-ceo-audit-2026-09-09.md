# Service Discovery deck — CEO audit, 9 September 2026

Deck audited: `docs/plans/MoSJE-Service-Discovery-Options.pptx`, 11 slides, as rebuilt at 13:20 on 9 September.
Posture: HOLD SCOPE. The deck's shape (one idea, three parts of the site, six options, a neutral summary) was set by the reviewer's feedback and is not re-litigated here. This audit asks what a decision-maker sees, what they cannot decide from it, and what would make them trust it.

## 1. What the deck does well

- **One idea, then the options.** Slide 2 puts the two lists first. Every later slide is a rearrangement of those lists, and the viewer can feel that. This is the right spine.
- **Every option is shown being used**, not described. Five of the six options carry a recording of a real page; the sixth is the page as it is today.
- **Same groups, same order, same labels** on every page and in the deck. A reviewer cannot catch the deck disagreeing with itself on who the Department serves.
- **No recommendation.** As instructed. The summary lays the six side by side and stops.
- **No numbers anywhere.** No counts, no eligibility claims, no unverified links. Nothing on a slide can be challenged as a fact the Department did not publish.
