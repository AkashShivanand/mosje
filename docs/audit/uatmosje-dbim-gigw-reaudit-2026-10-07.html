This report has two parts. Part 1 rechecks, one by one, the 54 points raised by Levithan Technologies in its DBIM and GIGW Level-1 reports of 1 Oct 2026. Part 2 lists new issues found on UAT during the recheck; they are kept apart so they do not mix with the agency's list.

Part 1

Recheck of the Agency's Findings

Not ready to pass. 21 of the 54 findings are closed on UAT.

UAT fixes most of the visual and accessibility engineering: the DBIM Blue palette, the footer, headings, labels, focus, zoom, pause controls, Feedback and Help. What remains is mostly content and governance: placeholder text, spelling, wrong office maps, missing Tender IDs, editable and scanned documents, the unpublished website policies, the Web Information Manager, and the chat button covering text.

18Partly fixed: some remains
13Fail: unchanged

DBIM: 8 pass, 9 partly, 7 fail. GIGW: 13 pass, 9 partly, 6 fail, 2 unverified. A finding passes only when nothing it describes can still be found on UAT.

How UAT Was Tested

  • 190 pages swept in Chrome at 1440 px: all 117 standard pages, every URL either report names, and four pages of each content type. axe-core 4.13 plus checks for headings, alt text, fonts, icon sizes, image weights, download labels, table alignment, footer colour, metadata and the quoted misspellings.
  • By hand on Home and About Us: keyboard, 200% and 400% zoom, phone width, print, styles off, the chat button at four widths, an external link, hover and contrast.
  • Content on Contact Us (all 19 organisations), Feedback, Help, Website Policies, Tenders, Videos, NCSK and four issued PDFs.
