Service Discovery — Before and After, Second Pass

The afternoon of 9 September 2026: the disability route removed from every surface, the deck rebuilt as a live presentation, the concept slide redrawn, and Option A of the home page on a still. Before images were captured before any edit, from the same place at the same size.

1 · The concept slide — no cross, no unproven claim

The × read as an error. The footer's claim that no scheme divides on stage, State or gender was not true as written, so it is gone. The disability sentence is gone. What remains is what can be defended: the two lists, and that every scheme is tagged on both.

2 · Option A of the home page — a still, not a recording

The recording did not play reliably, so the page shows the panel as it stands, cropped to the section.

3 · An option page — fewer words, the explanation in the notes

A page that is presented carries the screen and a handful of lines. What the presenter says is in the speaker notes.

4 · The closing slides

Five preparatory items became three; five safeguards became three rules; the disability safeguard is gone.

5 · The finder prototype — eleven groups and nothing beneath them

The dashed row for persons with disabilities beneath the grid, and the DEPwD result it led to, are removed.

6 · The design-system finder and the assistant

Both offered the disability choice in their first question. Neither does now.

7 · Figma

The concept frame's copy matches the slide and the × column is gone. The disability row is removed from the Option B question frame, the annotation bullet from Option B's notes, and the chip from the assistant frame.
