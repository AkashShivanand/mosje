UAT Content Fix List

From the UAT re-audit of 7 Oct 2026 against the Levithan DBIM and GIGW Level-1 reports (1 Oct 2026)

These are the open items from both parts of the re-audit that are fixed by editing pages, files and images in the CMS: the agency's findings (Part 1) and the new issues found on UAT (Part 2). Tasks that answer a new issue carry a Part 2 tag. None of them needs a developer. Each task names the UAT pages, what to do, and how to check it is done. Work on UAT first; the agency re-tests the build that goes live.

19tasks for the content team
6quick fixes, under 15 minutes each
6answer a Part 2 new issue

Limits to Work To

  • Images: logos and thumbnails 100 KB or less; banners, header and background images 500 KB or less; nothing over 5 MB. Save as WEBP, JPEG or PNG at the size the page shows it (DBIM 6.1.1).
  • Documents: PDF only, with real selectable text (not a scan), tagged for accessibility. No Word, Excel or PowerPoint files (DBIM A.5.6).
  • Download links: every link to a file says its format and size, for example "Annual Report 2025-26 (PDF, 6.6 MB)" (GIGW).
  • Text: paste as plain text so the site's Noto Sans font is kept (DBIM 4.1).
