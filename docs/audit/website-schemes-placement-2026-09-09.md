> renders at `/website/schemes-services`.

**Two out of every three entries in the Department's Schemes list are not schemes of the
Department.** The largest single group — 46 records — are **State Government schemes**,
mostly Maharashtra's, Madhya Pradesh's, Tamil Nadu's and Rajasthan's, catalogued without
naming their State. A citizen reading this page cannot tell which government they would be
applying to.

## 3. What is wrong with the 48 that do belong

Being a real scheme is not the same as being correctly presented. Among the 48:

- **Five are the same scheme listed twice** — National Overseas Scholarship under two names;
  Free Coaching under its own name and under "Dr. Ambedkar Centre of Excellence"; geriatric
  caregiver training as "Geriatric Caregivers Training" and as "PM Special"; PM-YASASVI in
  full and in acronym; and the pre-matric scholarship for children in unclean occupations,
  which is a component of the Pre-Matric SC scheme and has an empty page of its own.
- **Sub-schemes are listed as siblings of their own parents.** PM-YASASVI's five sub-schemes,
  SEED's four components, SMILE's two sub-schemes and AVYAY's components all sit flat in the
  same list as the umbrella they belong to. The list therefore looks larger than the
  programme actually is, and a reader cannot see which is inside which.
- **Nine Dr. Ambedkar Foundation and Babu Jagjivan Ram National Foundation schemes** are real
  Department schemes, but they are the Foundations' — they read better under those
  organisations than mixed into the Department's own Central Sector list.
- **Four pages are a documents table and nothing else** — Interest Subsidy for OBCs/EBCs,
  SHREYAS (OBC), Top Class Education for OBC/EBC/DNT, and the National Awards for prevention
  of alcoholism. A citizen gets a PDF and no answer.
- **Four pages are a pasted PDF.** The Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens page is
  **157 KB** of guideline text; Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana is 40 KB; SEED Free Coaching 24 KB;
  the DACE coaching page 23 KB.
- **PM-DAKSH** is recorded in the Demand for Grants 2026-27 as merging into PM Kaushal Vikas
  Yojana. It should not be shown as open without the Department confirming it.

## 5. What we recommend, in order

1. **Split the list into three, and label each.** The Department's own schemes; the
   corporations' loan products, under each corporation; State schemes, under DWBDNC with the
   State named. One list of 140 mixed items cannot be made trustworthy by sorting it.
2. **Collapse the umbrella schemes.** PM-YASASVI, SEED, SMILE, AVYAY and the SHREYAS family
   become parents with their sub-schemes inside them. The count falls and the truth rises.
3. **Move the 12 documents, tables and lists off the scheme list** into Acts & Rules,
   Publications, MoU and the Dashboard, and link each from the scheme it belongs to.
4. **Delete the four organisation duplicates** and redirect them to the organisation pages
   that already exist.
5. **Fill or withdraw the 22 empty pages.** An empty page published under a scheme name is
   worse than no page.
6. **Take the six unconfirmed records to the Department** — Credit Enhancement Guarantee
   Scheme, Upgradation of Merit of SC Students, SRMS, the PM CARES scholarship, "Awareness
   Program and Grant" and "Technology Upgradation of Clusters".
7. **Fix the placeholder helpline and the six copied standfirsts before anything else** —
   these are wrong facts on a live Government of India page, not presentation issues.

## 7. Method, and what this does not claim

Each record was read from the scraped content — its title, category, target group and body
text. A record was counted as a **scheme of this Department** only where it appears in the
Annual Report 2025-26 Chapter 3 or carries a line in Demand No. 93 of the Notes on Demands
for Grants 2026-27, or where it is a published scheme of the Dr. Ambedkar Foundation or the
Babu Jagjivan Ram National Foundation, both bodies of this Department.

**State attribution is stated only where the record itself says so** — "MP", "Tamil Nadu",
the VJNT/SBC categories that only Maharashtra uses, the MBC category that only Tamil Nadu
uses, the Gadia Lohar community of Rajasthan. Where the wording is a State's but the State
is not named, the row says "State scheme" without naming one. Confirming each State is work
for the Department, not for a reading of the page.

This pass did not open the source PDFs attached to each record, and did not check whether a
scheme is still accepting applications. It reads what the site publishes.
