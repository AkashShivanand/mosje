Service Discovery — Before and After

What the 8 September 2026 review changed, shown from the same place at the same size. Before images were captured before any edit; after images from the rebuilt prototypes, the new Figma frames and the rebuilt deck.

1 · The finder — five questions to two

The review dropped "who is this for", stage of life and State. None of the Department's schemes divides on them. The running "45 of 134 still match" counter is gone; the persona list is the Department's own mandate, opening on Students.

2 · One tap — persona first, grouped by what the Department provides

The "128 active schemes" and "12 active schemes" figures are off. Rows are grouped by offering, each names the place to apply and links to the scheme page, which carries the portal.

3 · The persona panel opens on Students

The review asked that the sequence open on a female figure. The panel now reads the same eleven personas as every other option.

4 · Schemes page, pictures and cards — two filters

Persona and what it provides, and no third filter. Cards are paged; no count is printed.

5 · Schemes page, filter panel and table — no numbers

The per-filter counts and the "19 schemes" header are gone; a filter that would leave nothing is greyed. The table carries what each scheme provides, whom it names and its type.

5a · The finder's answers — the group, and the disability route

Added in the 9 September audit. Every card reads For and Provides, names where to apply and links to the scheme's page. A person who chooses Persons with Disabilities is shown DEPwD's four schemes, labelled as DEPwD's, rather than a note and nothing.

6 · The assistant and the design-system finder — new, no before

Both now ask the same two questions over the same scheme master. The assistant screen is the design system's own Chatbot; the finder is a website section built from design-system components.

7 · Figma — the option frames

The earlier frame is kept and renamed as superseded. The new section is grouped as the review asked: concept, home page, Schemes page, assistant, placement. Every fill, text and radius is bound to a SAMAVESH style or variable.

8 · The deck — title and opening

The "134 schemes published" figure is off the title. The second slide is now the concept: the citizen on the left, the Department on the right.
