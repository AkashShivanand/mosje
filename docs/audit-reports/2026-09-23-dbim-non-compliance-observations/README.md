## Verification, 23 September 2026

Every observation was checked against DBIM 3.0 and against the live site:

| Verdict | Count | Observations |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed defect | 7 | 1, 5, 6, 9, 16, 17, 20 |
| Valid as placement or wording only | 6 | 3, 7 (position), 8, 10, 12, 14 |
| Not a DBIM requirement — measured against an illustrative figure | 4 | 4, 13, 15, 18 |
| Factually wrong about what the site does | 2 | 2, 11 (and the WhatsApp half of 7) |
| Reference, not an observation | 1 | 19 |

The four that matter most: **no PM quote section**, **no announcements ticker**,
**86 of 96 homepage images carry empty alternative text**, and the brand blue
**#0373DF appears in no DBIM primary colour group**.

The two to contest: observation 2 claims Header 2 is in use when the site carries
Logo Lockup 2, which is what Header 1 prescribes; observation 11 calls Important
Links missing when it is present as a fixed right-edge control.

## Gaps this report missed, found while checking it

- The header is duplicated in the markup, so a keyboard user traverses about 23
  invisible controls a second time.
- 81 of 228 keyboard-reachable elements have no accessible name, including the
  site search field.
- The visible focus ring comes from the third-party UserWay widget, not our own
  stylesheet.
- The footer is missing Archives and Feedback, both named in DBIM Table 12.
- Department is missing three Level-2 sections: Our Team, Our Groups, Our Performance.
