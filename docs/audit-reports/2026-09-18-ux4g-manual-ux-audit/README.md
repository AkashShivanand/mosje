## What it is

A **human** review, not the automated Audit 360 tool. It evaluates the site
against Jakob Nielsen's usability heuristics and industry UX principles across
seven areas: user flow and navigation, visual design and layout, interaction
design, features, accessibility, UX writing, and UX/UI principles.

## The four challenges it names

1. **Usability and navigation** — complex navigation and information-heavy
   sections make it hard for a first-time user to find a service and finish a task.
2. **Accessibility and inclusivity** — dense content and inconsistent hierarchy,
   typography and interaction patterns create barriers for elderly users, users
   with disabilities and users with low digital literacy.
3. **Content and clarity** — long-form content, technical terminology and
   inconsistent labels make important information hard to scan.
4. **Mobile experience** — content density, excessive scrolling, and small or
   crowded targets.
