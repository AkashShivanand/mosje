## In the register

All 30 are folded in under the source `levithan-gigw-2026-10`. Twenty-three
attach to rows that already existed; seven were new — content overlapping with
CSS off, mixed Hindi and English on a page, no notice when leaving the site, the
website address missing from issued documents, seven unpublished policies, icons
unreachable by keyboard, and focus lost at the end of the page.

## Two things to carry into any reply

**Five findings cannot be closed by the delivery team.** 8.3 and 8.12 need the
Department to name its key functionaries and its Web Information Manager; 8.5
needs approved Help text; 8.13 is a change to the Department's letterhead, not to
the website; and 8.16 needs captions for 69 YouTube and 30 uploaded videos.

**8.29 cannot be closed by code alone.** "Some content is not properly conveyed
by screen reader" is answered in the fix sheet by pointing at 8.22, 8.26 and
8.28 being fixed — but the claim is about what a screen reader announces, and
only a screen-reader pass can settle it.
