Filters and Officer Login — Proposal

Filters change which figures you see; Officer Login changes who you are. So the login leaves the filter row for the page banner, and each page shows only the filters its figures can answer.

Dashboard

Type of Applicant removed; State/UT stays. Officer Login moves to the page banner, on the title line. No year filter here: the cards cover different periods.

SMILE-Beggary

State/UT, then District (active once a State is chosen — SMILE publishes by district), then Financial Year.

e-Utthaan

Financial Year: allocations are published year by year. No District: DAPSC is All-India only.

Where Each Filter Applies

State/UT: every page; a dashboard that publishes All-India figures only keeps saying so. District: SMILE-Beggary only, once a State is chosen. Financial Year: SMILE-Beggary, e-Utthaan, e-Anudaan and Senior Citizens Welfare. None on NMBA (cumulative since launch) or the Department (its published periods). Type of Applicant: removed everywhere.
