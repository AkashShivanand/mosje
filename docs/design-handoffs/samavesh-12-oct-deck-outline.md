**Speaker notes (verbatim)**

> The spine is the Grant-in-Aid lifecycle. Each of the ten value additions is placed at the stage where it acts.
>
> **1 Application.** NGO Darpan validates the applicant against the NITI Aayog register at entry; multilingual support covers 22 languages; OCR reads and checks mandatory uploads before submission is allowed, cutting the deficiency loop.
>
> **2 Scrutiny.** RBAC confines each officer to the actions permitted for their role; UDISE and NICSI integration validates institution details against the source system instead of self-declaration.
>
> **3 Inspection.** The e-Inspection module allows premises to be viewed remotely via CCTV; video conferencing runs the NGO call alongside the feed; a single standard report format replaces scheme-wise formats.
>
> **4 Release and monitoring.** PFMS two-way integration pushes the sanction for payment and returns payment status, removing manual re-entry by the AO/PO team; the scheme-wise dashboard gives live status in one view.
>

## Points to resolve before the rebuild (not part of the deck)

These were found in the source. They have **not** been corrected above.

1. **Slide 4: the notes do not match the slide.** The notes describe "ten value additions" placed on a four-stage Grant-in-Aid lifecycle, and refer to items 05, 06, 07 and 09. The slide has a single-column list of 17 integrations, with no lifecycle and no numbering that matches. The notes seem to belong to an earlier version of this slide.
2. **The same name is spelt three ways:** "Tele-MANAS" (slide 3), "Telamanas" (slide 4) and "Telemanas".
3. **Spelling and capitalisation on slide 4:** "Bharat maps" should be "Bharat Maps"; "NAtional Career Services" should be "National Career Services"; "Aishe" should be "AISHE"; "Digital signature certificate (DCS)" should be "Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)".
4. **Slide 5 says WCAG 2.0/2.1.** The estate targets WCAG 2.2 AA, so this may undersell the work.
5. **Slide 2 heading** uses a hyphen ("SAMAVESH - One Digital Platform"). An em dash, as used on slide 3, would be consistent.
6. **The "31 Portals" figure** cannot be checked against the deck itself. The project brief lists 20 workflow portals plus the website, covering 33+ organisations and schemes. Confirm the number before presenting.
