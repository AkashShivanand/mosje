Hero Banner — Samavesh Utsav

A fourth banner for the home-page hero, built from the lamp-lighting photograph of Samavesh Utsav. It uses the treatment already approved for Hero 01, 02 and 03 — a text panel beside the photograph, rather than type over it. Every line of copy has been checked against the event's own standee and against the Board's website.

One line was wrong and has been fixed. The descriptor originally read “Denotified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Communities” — my own translation of the Hindi on the standee. The Board's own English name, on dwbdnc.dosje.gov.in and in this estate's organisation registry, is “De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Communities”: hyphen after De, and a comma before “and”. The banner now carries the Department's form.
